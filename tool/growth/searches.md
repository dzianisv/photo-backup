search of Google Play listings + AppBrain mirrors. Re-score after

"Volume: how to read this").

## Volume: how to read this

- Google Play publishes no search-volume numbers, and no free
  public source gives Play keyword volume (checked: Play search
  results via web search, AppBrain app pages). Paid ASO tools
  (Sensor Tower etc.) are the only volume source — not used here.
- Demand proxy used instead: installs of the niche's direct apps.
  Photos to USB: OTG Backup — 270 installs in ~40 days since
  Oct 2026 launch (AppBrain). VaultDrive: USB Photo Backup —
  870 installs total, ~21/day over the last 30 days (AppBrain,
  Nov 14, 2026). The niche has real but small demand; two new
  direct rivals launched Oct 2026 and are both still unrated.
- Difficulty is qualitative, from the size/ratings of the apps
  ranking on top for that query on Nov 14, 2026.
