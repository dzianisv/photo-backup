SnapDisk Privacy Policy

SnapDisk is an offline, on-device backup app. This policy explains what the app does — and does not — collect.

Last updated: November 14, 2026

TL;DR: Nothing. SnapDisk has no account system, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no tracking. It does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. Your photos are never uploaded, shared, or sent anywhere.

What the app collects

Nothing. SnapDisk has no account system, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no tracking. It does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.

What happens to your photos

Copying runs entirely on your device, from your internal storage to the external disk you choose. Your photos and videos are never uploaded, shared, or sent anywhere. Folder choices (From / To) and the Pro purchase state are stored only on your device, in the app's own preferences.

Permissions

  • Storage / media access: to read the photos and videos you back up and write them to the disk you choose. The app reads only photos and videos, oldest first.
  • Notifications: to offer a backup when a disk is connected and to show copy progress.
  • Foreground service: to keep the copy running in the background.
  • No internet permission: the app cannot access the network.

Purchases

SnapDisk Pro is a one-time purchase ($9.99) handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; SnapDisk itself receives no payment details and sends nothing anywhere.

Third parties

None, except Google Play for the optional Pro purchase. The app talks to no other third-party service.

Rule of thumb: if the backup works with the phone in airplane mode, it is offline. Try it — SnapDisk copies with no connection at all, because there is nothing to connect to.
